Implementing a 'Pre ToolUse' check ensures that the business logic is handled in the application layer, providing a 100% guarantee of compliance and security. If the message is off-topic, the classifier can trigger a hard-coded rejection response or redirect the user, bypassing the expensive main agent entirely. This pattern drastically reduces operational costs, mitigates the risk of prompt injection, and ensures the main agent's context window is reserved for high-value support interactions.
